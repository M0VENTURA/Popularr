Escalating wait between searches when a track finds no results. 2 · Star Ratings

After 5★ tracks (confirmed singles and genuine standouts) are set, the rest of the album is rated purely by where each track falls in the album's own popularity distribution — no artist context is consulted. A track lands in the first band its album z-score clears. These bands form a continuous ladder, so 2★ automatically sits between 3★ and 1★:

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5★
