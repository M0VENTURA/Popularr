Step 1 — Get your playlist CSV

Visit exportify.net, log in with Spotify, and click Export next to any playlist. Save the .csv file, then upload it below — no Spotify credentials are needed.

Open exportify.net
Step 2 — Upload your CSV
Playlist mode matches each CSV track against your library and builds a Navidrome playlist. Album mode queues every track into the download queue as ONE new album. A single-artist CSV becomes that artist's album; mixed artists become Various Artists. "Do not search album release" is applied automatically, and tracks already in your collection are copied instead of downloaded.
Exportify CSVs work as-is. Manual CSVs are fine too — only Track Name and Artist Name(s) are required.
CSV column guide (Exportify & manual)

Columns are matched by name, case-insensitively. Only two are required:

  1. Track Name (also accepts Name, Title)
  2. Artist Name(s) (also accepts Artist, Artists)

Optional columns that improve matching, album import and duration checks:

Album NameAlbum / Album — used for album grouping and matching.
Album ArtistAlbum Artist — decides single-artist vs Various Artists.
Duration (ms)Milliseconds — enables the library-copy check and length verification. Plain duration is treated as milliseconds.
ISRCExact library match where available.
Release DateYear for the imported album.
GenresCarried into the queue metadata.
Track URISpotify id (Exportify) — kept for reference.

Any other Exportify columns (Danceability, Energy, Tempo…) are ignored.

The download queue will fetch these tracks, copy any you already own, and import them as one album. "Do not search album release" is set automatically once they import.
